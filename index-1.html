[07]Answers_

Frequently asked questions.

01Is the price really for the whole team?

Yes. SME is $100 a month for 10 to 20 staff with 3 admins. Enterprise is $189 a month for up to 50 staff. There's no per-seat charge on either.

02Is our data mixed with other companies?

No. Every company on Now runs on its own separate database, under its own address.

03Does it work on phones?

Yes. Now runs in the browser and is built for mobile, so people can clock in, apply for leave and check payslips from their phone. Light and dark mode included.

04What happens when someone forgets to clock out?

Now checks for open shifts every 15 minutes. A shift left open too long is closed automatically and flagged, so reports show a missed clock-out instead of a 40-hour day.

05Who can see what?

There are four levels. Employees see their own stuff. Department heads see their team. Heads of operations and admins see the whole company, and those logins are protected with two-factor authentication.

06We have more than 50 people. Now what?

That's the Custom plan. Send your details through the form, pick Custom, and the sales team will work out a plan with you.
